The problem

Peer-to-peer gig work runs on trust nobody verifies. A freelancer agrees a price in a Fiverr DM, does the work, and hopes. Escrow is too slow and expensive for a $250 job; chat platforms offer zero financial protection. Fraud is conversational — it happens in the negotiation, where no software is watching.

What Aegis does

An autonomous AI voice arbitrator that sits inside the negotiation as a neutral third party. Two people talk. Aegis drafts the escrow contract from what they actually said, holds the funds, catches the manipulation as it is spoken, verifies the delivered work, and settles disputes — with no human ever reading the case.

1  Voice Escrow

Terms extracted live from speech over the OpenAI Realtime API and published to both screens as they are heard, so a misheard price surfaces before money is committed. Funds lock only on two separate spoken agreements.

2  Deception Forensics

Six fraud patterns scored as spoken — off-platform payment, late price change, manufactured urgency, scope creep, spoofing, threats. Past a threshold Aegis interrupts with a Security Halt and refuses to lock.

3  The Trust Reel

A real narrated 1080×1920 video with burned-in subtitles, rendered in-browser. Figures are read from the ledger, so a reel cannot advertise a payout that never happened.

The Vocal Entropy Trap — defeating cloned voices

Before funds lock, Aegis issues a randomised phrase from a ~2.3 million phrase space ("The copper kettle counted nineteen umbrellas") and measures pure reaction time — from when Aegis stops speaking to when the party starts. A human answers in well under a second. A synthesis pipeline must transcribe, generate and re-render on text it has never seen, and the delay gives it away. The response is scored on phonetic similarity, so an honest mishearing does not fail a real person.

Advanced adjudication — the AI Jury

Disputes convene three agents: a Buyer's Advocate, a Seller's Advocate, and a Magistrate who issues a binding settlement in integer basis points. A $33.33 contract split 70/30 pays $23.33 and $10.00 — the buyer absorbs the rounding remainder, so the ledger nets to exactly zero.

Guardian — deals that started somewhere else

Most gig deals are agreed on Fiverr or Discord long before anyone considers escrow. Paste the conversation and Aegis runs the same six-pattern analysis, extracts the deal, and converts it into funded escrow via a share link — no voice room, and the counterparty needs no account until they accept. Findings must quote the source verbatim; the backend discards any quote it cannot locate in the pasted text.

Engineering that holds up

  • Money never touches a float. price_cents is authoritative; price_usd is a generated Postgres column derived from it, so displayed and settled amounts cannot drift.
  • Immutable ledger. Every movement runs through SECURITY DEFINER functions that row-lock, validate and append in one transaction. Triggers reject any UPDATE or DELETE on ledger entries.
  • Least privilege. Money functions are revoked from anon and authenticated. Only the backend calls them, and it re-validates every argument the model produces.
  • Three gates before any lock: risk score below threshold, a passed live-speaker challenge, and real buyer solvency.
  • Key safety. The OpenAI key never reaches the browser — WebRTC SDP is proxied through FastAPI.
  • Two-party remote voice via peer-to-peer audio bridged through the host and signalled over Supabase Realtime — no SFU.
